Le Conseil du Maître Joaillier

The Ethics of Precious Metal Preservation

True luxury respects the integrity of raw materials. Because abrasive mechanical lapping removes a microscopic layer of precious metal, LUMIÈRE advises limiting deep sculptural resurfacing to no more than three interventions across the lifetime of an heirloom bangle. Conversely, our complimentary Rouge de Paris Soft Buffing & Cavitation Ritual removes zero metal and may be indulged in prior to every gala or soirée.
